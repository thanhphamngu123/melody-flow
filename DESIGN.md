# Design System

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Visual World

Dark mode studio glassmorphic theme with HSL tailored emerald accents, Plus Jakarta Sans typography, and smooth GSAP ScrollTrigger batch animations.

## Color Tokens

- `--bg-primary`: `#040806`
- `--bg-secondary`: `#0a0f0c`
- `--accent`: `#10b981`
- `--accent-hover`: `#059669`
- `--text-primary`: `#ffffff`
- `--text-secondary`: `#cbd5e1`
- `--border`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`

## Typography

- Font Family: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif
- Headings: `800` weight, `-0.02em` tracking, white `#ffffff`
- Body: `15px - 18px`, high contrast `#cbd5e1`, `1.6` line-height
